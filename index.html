NetInsight Privacy Policy

Effective date: 7 November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the NetInsight Android application ("NetInsight", "the app", "we") handles information when you use it.

1. Information handled by the app

NetInsight reads network traffic counters provided by Android to display download and upload speeds. To show Wi-Fi and mobile-data usage, the app uses Android's network-usage statistics after you enable Usage Access in Android settings.

Daily Wi-Fi and mobile-data totals and related dates are stored in a local database on your device to display usage summaries and history. The app keeps up to 180 days of this history and removes older records automatically.

The app also uses Android notification permission, where required, to show its ongoing live-speed notification. It receives the device-boot event so its monitoring service can resume after a restart, subject to Android and device-manufacturer restrictions.

2. Speed test and third-party service

If you choose to run the in-app speed test, NetInsight connects to Cloudflare's speed-test service at speed.cloudflare.com to measure ping, download speed, and upload speed. Your device necessarily sends network requests to Cloudflare. Cloudflare may receive technical information such as your IP address, request details, and connection metadata, and handles that information under its own privacy terms: Cloudflare Privacy Policy.

The speed test is optional. Normal live speed monitoring and local usage-history display do not require running the speed test.

3. Advertising and Google Mobile Ads

NetInsight integrates the Google Mobile Ads SDK to display advertisements. The SDK may collect and process information such as the device's IP address, advertising or other device identifiers, app and ad interactions, diagnostic information, and approximate location inferred from the IP address, as permitted by your device settings and applicable law. Google may use this information to deliver, measure, and improve ads, prevent fraud, and provide related advertising services. The information is handled by Google under its own privacy policy: Google Privacy Policy.

Advertising may be personalized or non-personalized depending on your location, applicable consent requirements, and available device or account settings. Where required, the app should present Google's consent choices before requesting ads. Users can manage advertising and privacy settings through their Android device and Google account.

NetInsight does not receive or store your Google advertising profile. Google independently processes information collected through its advertising services.

4. Sharing and sale

NetInsight does not operate its own account system or app analytics service. The app's network counters and saved usage history are used on your device and are not intentionally uploaded by NetInsight to a developer-operated server. The third-party speed-test connection described above is an exception because it is needed to perform a test.

We do not sell personal information. We do not share locally stored usage history with advertisers.

5. Permissions

  • Internet: connects to the optional Cloudflare speed-test service.
  • Network state and Wi-Fi state: identifies the active network and reads network status.
  • Usage Access: reads Android-provided Wi-Fi and mobile-data usage statistics. You grant or revoke this access in Android settings.
  • Notifications: displays the ongoing speed and usage notification where Android requires permission.
  • Boot completed: allows the app to attempt to resume its monitoring service after device restart.
  • Foreground service: keeps the live speed monitor running with an ongoing notification.

6. Storage, retention, and deletion

Usage history is stored locally in the app's database and retained for up to 180 days. You can clear app storage or uninstall NetInsight through Android settings to remove the app's local data. Android device backup behavior may be governed by your device and Android backup settings.

7. Children's privacy

NetInsight is a general-purpose network utility and is not designed to collect personal information from children. The app does not ask users to create an account or submit personal details.

8. Security

We aim to limit information handling to what is needed for the app's functions. No method of storage or transmission is completely risk-free. Protect your device and review Android permissions and backup settings.

9. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when the app's features or data practices change. The effective date at the top will be revised when changes are made.
